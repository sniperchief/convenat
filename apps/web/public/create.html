Compile a condition

Describe the condition in plain language. The compiler turns it into a deterministic specification — success and failure conditions, a deadline, approved sources, ambiguities and edge cases — and hashes it. The model never chooses the economics. The token, the trading window, the challenge window and the bond come from operator policy, so approving the hash cannot approve terms a model invented.

4 · Approve and register

        

          Registering stores the specification against its hash. It does not create the
          market: your own wallet commits the hash on-chain by calling the factory, and the indexer
          joins the two when it observes MarketCreated. The backend never opens a
          market on your behalf.
